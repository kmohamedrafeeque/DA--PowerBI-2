# Air Quality of Delhi 
I developed an Air Quality of Delhi dashboard using Power BI to analyze air pollution levels and trends across different time periods. The project focuses on key air quality indicators, helping identify pollution patterns, seasonal variations, and overall air quality conditions in Delhi. With interactive visuals and clear metrics, the dashboard supports data-driven understanding of environmental health and can assist policymakers and citizens in interpreting air quality data effectively.
